kp_nl (sched) 0.8

Trim ° 0
Wheels ON the ground. 1) Hold the robot vertical and click Calibrate upright (sets tilt zero). 2) Click Enable balancing and let go. Defaults are ELEGOO's factory gains (kp_balance 55, kd_balance 0.75, kp_speed 10, ki_speed 0.26). If it drives the wrong way when tilted, flip MOTOR_SIGN in the firmware and reflash. A tilt past 22° auto-stops the motors (pick-up detection).
